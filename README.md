# 🌑 SINKHOLE — Short Film

> *When the ground opens… it doesn’t just swallow bodies.*
> *It consumes truths.*

---

## 🎬 About

**SINKHOLE** is a psychological short film created for the **Percepta Film Competition**.
Blending minimalism with tension, the film explores what lies beneath—not just physically, but emotionally and mentally.

This isn’t just a disaster story.
It’s about collapse—of reality, identity, and control.

---

## 🌌 Concept

A sudden sinkhole appears.
Silent. Unexplainable. Watching.

But as people gather around it…
something deeper begins to surface.

> The real horror isn’t falling in—
> it’s what calls you down.
